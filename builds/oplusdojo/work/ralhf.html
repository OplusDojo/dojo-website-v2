Overview

Your AI is only as good as the information you feed it. RaLHF collects the context people choose, keeps it fresh, and feeds it to the AI they already use.

The challenge

Personal AI assistants start every conversation knowing almost nothing about you: what you buy, where you go, what you enjoy. People end up explaining their background again and again.

RaLHF set out to give people a history they own, collected from the accounts and files they choose, and to make it useful to any AI they use now or in years to come.

The product

  • Collects context from the web apps, files, folders and email people choose
  • Keeps that context fresh as life moves on
  • Feeds it to the AI assistants people already use
  • Puts people in control of what their AI can access
The product

Your subscriptions, travel, taste and choices, turned into context your AI can use.
