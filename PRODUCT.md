## Users

- **Primary: founders, entrepreneurs and companies who need a complex product or AI system built and run in production.** They are deciding whether to trust an outside team with something they cannot afford to fail. Worldwide, with emphasis on emerging markets and South America, and North America.
- **Investors**, who judge whether O+Dojo is a credible, well-led company that ships real systems.
- **Engineers considering joining** (Careers link, careers@oplusdojo.com).

The visitor's job on the site: decide that this team can be trusted with their system, then book a call.

## Product Purpose

O+Dojo (OPlusDojo) is an agentic AI and cloud engineering firm. It designs, builds, secures and runs complex systems for clients, and stays accountable for them in production.

Success: a visitor finishes believing "a senior North American team of former FAANG engineers will own our complex system, put us first, and take it all the way to production", and books a call.

## Product Principles

1. **Proof over adjectives.** Every claim of experience or quality points at a real employer, person, figure or client result.
2. **The client comes first.** Copy starts from the client's goal and situation, then shows how O+Dojo gets them there.
3. **Ownership to production.** The promise is accountability from first call to a running, monitored system, not a prototype.
4. **Senior and reachable.** Clients work directly with the experienced people who design their system.
5. **Globally legible.** Plain, confident English that reads as premium to a non-native reader.
